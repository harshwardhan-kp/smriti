THE FEATURE
A floating bubble sits over other apps. LONG-PRESS it and two things happen at once:
a screenshot of whatever was on screen is taken, and voice recording starts. On RELEASE,
recording stops and a record is saved with that screenshot as its photo and the transcript as
its text - exactly like a normal capture, then enriched in the background like any other.
A short TAP does nothing destructive: just show a brief hint that they should hold to capture.

2. CREATE app/src/main/java/com/smriti/app/capture/BubbleService.kt
   A Service that adds a small draggable overlay View via WindowManager with
   TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY, FLAG_NOT_FOCUSABLE, and a translucent circular amber drawable
   matching the app's shutter (see ui/theme for ColorAmber).
   Gesture handling on the overlay, via a raw OnTouchListener (NOT Compose - this is a
   WindowManager view):
     - ACTION_DOWN: record the down time and position. Start a Handler postDelayed of 400 ms
       that, if not cancelled, begins the capture: fire ScreenCaptureService ACTION_CAPTURE and
       start voice recording. Visually grow/brighten the bubble so the user can see it is live.
     - ACTION_MOVE beyond touch slop: treat as a DRAG, cancel the pending long-press, and move
       the overlay via WindowManager.updateViewLayout. Persist the last position in
       SharedPreferences so it stays where the user left it.
     - ACTION_UP: if the long-press had fired, stop recording and complete the capture. If not,
       it was a tap - show a short Toast "Hold to capture" and do nothing else.
   IMPORTANT ORDERING: request the screenshot FIRST, before starting the recorder. The
   screenshot must show the screen the user was looking at when they pressed, not the app after
   it has been disturbed.
