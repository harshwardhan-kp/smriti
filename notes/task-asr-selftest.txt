Add an ASR verification hook to this Android/Kotlin repo. Edit/create files directly. ADDITIVE ONLY.

WHY
SherpaWhisperAsr was just added. It compiles and its unit tests pass, but NOTHING has proven it
actually transcribes audio on the device. Its failure mode is silent: a wrong endianness or a
wrong normalisation divisor in the WAV-to-FloatArray step yields silence or noise, not an
exception. The only way to catch that is to run known audio through the real native path and
compare against a known transcript.

Reference WAVs are already on the device at /data/local/tmp/asr/test_wavs/ (0.wav, 1.wav) —
mono, 16 kHz, 16-bit, the same format AudioRecorder produces.
