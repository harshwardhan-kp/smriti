DECISION (already made, do not revisit)
Capture no longer runs OCR. The photo is kept purely as evidence. The voice transcript becomes
the ONLY text input to extraction. ML Kit goes away entirely.

WHY THIS MATTERS BEYOND TIDINESS
ML Kit's transitive dependency com.google.android.datatransport:transport-backend-cct declares
INTERNET in its own manifest, which the manifest merger silently added to this app. That is why
app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml carries these two lines:
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.INTERNET" tools:node="remove" />
    <uses-permission android:name="android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE" tools:node="remove" />
Removing ML Kit may make those unnecessary. DO NOT delete them in this task — the build task
:app:assertNoNetworkPermission is what proves whether they are still needed, and we will test
that separately. Leave the manifest completely untouched.
